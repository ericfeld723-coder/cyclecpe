Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about IRS Enrolled Agent continuing education requirements, reporting, and platform features.

What are the continuing education (CE) requirements for an Enrolled Agent?

Enrolled agents must complete 72 hours of qualifying continuing education every 3-year enrollment cycle. A minimum of 16 hours must be completed each enrollment year, and at least 2 of those annual hours must cover ethics or professional conduct (Circular 230).

How are certificates of completion generated?

Once you successfully pass a course's final examination with a passing grade of 70% or higher, your course completion certificate is automatically generated inside the course view tab, complete with your credit hours, completion date, and sponsor details ready for download.

How does Form 8554 auto-sync work?

Form 8554 (Application for Renewal of Enrollment to Practice Before the Internal Revenue Service) details are automatically updated whenever you modify your practitioner profile information or complete new CE courses on the CPE Credit tab, ensuring seamless recordkeeping.

What records must I retain for IRS audit purposes?

Enrolled agents are required to retain certificates of completion, course outlines, and graded examination results for a minimum of four (4) years following the date of renewal.

Are new tax law updates like OBBBA incorporated into the curriculum?

Yes. All self-study courses are fully updated to incorporate current IRS guidance, including provisions from the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA) and Revenue Procedure figures.

Can non-credentialed preparers use this catalog?

Yes, non-credentialed preparers seeking an Annual Filing Season Program (AFSP) Record of Completion can utilize dedicated catalog courses such as the Annual Federal Tax Refresher (AFTR).
