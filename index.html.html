Enrolled Agent Frequently Asked Questions & IRS Compliance Guidelines

Comprehensive guidance sourced directly from official IRS resources. Browse or search below for answers regarding exams, continuing education, renewals, and missed deadlines.

1. What is an Enrolled Agent and what are their representation rights? ▼
An enrolled agent (EA) is a person who has earned the privilege of representing taxpayers before the Internal Revenue Service. Enrolled agents have unlimited representation rights.
2. How do you become an enrolled agent? ▼
Obtain a PTIN, pass all three parts of the Special Enrollment Examination (SEE) within three years, apply via Form 23, and pass suitability checks.
3. What are the Continuing Education (CE) requirements for Enrolled Agents? ▼
72 hours of qualifying continuing education every three-year renewal cycle, including a minimum of 16 hours per year and at least 2 hours of ethics each year.
4. When is my EA renewal due and how is the cycle determined? ▼
Every three years based on the last digit of your SSN according to IRS Publication 5186, submitted via Pay.gov Form 8554.
5. What happens if I miss my renewal deadline or have a CPE/CE deficit? ▼
Your enrollment becomes inactive, and you lose representation rights until makeup CE hours are completed and reinstatement is approved.
